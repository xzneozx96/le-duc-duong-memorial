## Prompt 1: desktop (landscape, 16:9, 2560 × 1440 px)

Create a photorealistic memorial hero image in the style of image 1: a warm, soft, golden-hour atmosphere with a hazy sky, gentle bokeh light, a calm river or lake at sunset in the far background, a few soft white wildflowers and one lit candle in the lower foreground, and old-style photo prints with thin white borders scattered at the edges, slightly tilted and overlapping. The colours are warm sepia, cream, sand and soft gold, with low contrast and a nostalgic, peaceful, dignified mood.

The main subject is the elderly Vietnamese man from images 2 and 3. He must be exactly that person: keep his face shape, eyes, nose, mouth and smile, his wrinkles and skin tone, his short grey hair brushed straight up, his hairline, and his ears exactly as in image 2. He wears NO glasses. Keep his beige suit jacket, white shirt and patterned brown tie from image 2. Show him from the chest up, his face turned at most slightly to the side with a gentle, warm smile, softly lit by the sunset light. Do not age him, slim him, beautify him or change his features. If you must choose between style and likeness, choose likeness.

Composition: place him in the right half of the frame, his face at about two thirds of the width and slightly above the vertical centre. Keep the left 45% of the image calm and open (soft sky, light haze, the distant water) with no objects, no faces and no busy detail, because the website will place its own text there.

The small old-style prints: use images 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 as the photos inside the prints, exactly as they are, without redrawing or inventing faces. Show 4 to 6 of them as small prints with thin white borders, toned slightly sepia, scattered along the right edge and the lower right corner, partly fading into the haze, as in image 1. Image 4 (him as a young man) should be the most visible print, in the lower area near the candle.

Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, captions, logos, signatures, watermarks or heart symbols anywhere in the image. No glasses on anyone. No extra people in the main scene. No religious symbols.

## Tips

- **If the face drifts**, regenerate with this line added at the start: "Identity is the top priority: the man must be recognisable to his family as the man in image 2."
- **If the small prints come out with redrawn or invented faces**, generate a clean version without prints (replace that paragraph with "No photo prints in the image"). The website can then place the real photos over the image as framed prints, so every face in them is authentic.
- **Before publishing**, have the family check the face, so he is shown as they remember him.
