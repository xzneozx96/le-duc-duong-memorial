# The memorial site uses the night-train look, amber on actions, and family wording

- Date: 2026-11-18
- Status: **decided on 2026-11-18** (the developer, for the family, in chat during the design of item 001). Plan only: nothing is built yet beyond the lab page `/lab/memorial/b`.
- Changes: `DESIGN.md`, `.factory/work/001-memorial-website/spec.md` (Constraints line on the visual direction), `.factory/work/001-memorial-website/design/ux.md` and `EXPERIENCE.md`.
- Related: item 001-memorial-website, design decisions U1, U15, U19, U20 and U21.

## Why

The brainstorm chose a warm paper album look. In the first lab build the three proposed directions looked almost the same, so they were redrawn as three clearly different looks and built as lab pages: A the family album, B the night train, C Hà Nội daylight. The family then picked one, and settled the wording the site uses to address visitors.

## Decisions

1. **Direction B, the night train.** A near-black warm page with warm light text, the black-and-white archive photos as full-width film frames, white enamel station signs hung from one rail, Archivo Narrow for his name, signs and controls, Spectral for reading text. Values are in `DESIGN.md`.
2. **Amber on the title and on actions.** The primary button, the play controls, text buttons and the lit rail bracket are amber with dark labels (9.9:1). Amber is never used on running text, years, headings, captions or meta.
3. **The German page translates the title.** "Ein Sonnenstrahl, der bleibt" (a draft for the German speaker to check); the Vietnamese page keeps "Một ánh Dương còn mãi".
4. **Vietnamese text addresses visitors as "bạn"**, with "xin" in requests.
5. **The memory wall is called "Lưu bút"** in Vietnamese and "Gästebuch" in German.
6. **The phone's first screen keeps the full portrait.** His name and the jump links follow after it (a design review suggested a shorter portrait; the family kept it).
7. **The intro under the header is at most two lines on a phone** in each language (the reading of AC-2's "two-line intro").

## Rejected

- **A, the family album.** Closest to the brainstorm, but read as the common warm-paper default.
- **C, Hà Nội daylight.** Bright and alive, but the archive years looked smaller than the recent ones.
- **Amber on the title only.** It left the buttons hard to spot on the dark page.
- **"quý vị" and "Sổ tưởng nhớ".** The family preferred the plainer "bạn" and "Lưu bút".
