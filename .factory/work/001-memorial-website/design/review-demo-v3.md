# Award-level review of demo v3 (design-reviewer, 2026-11-18)

Scores as an Awwwards jury: Design 5.5, Usability 7.5, Creativity 3.5, Content 7.5; about 5.5 overall, not near Site of the Day. The owner rated it 3/10. The gap is the concept, not polish: more effects on this layout would not close it.

What made it generic:
- Hero: the stock split hero (years, name, tagline, intro, links left; photo right). Cormorant's Vietnamese marks float off the letters at display size ("Lê", "Đức"). The name's mask leaves a huge gap between lines and slices letter tops at frame 0.
- Films: two dark text-only cards; the most moving material, his recorded voice, is the least visual element.
- Timeline: a standard alternating centre-line timeline; hairline boxes as signs; a thin line that breaks at every photo row; a 7 px "sun"; ragged left edges; a 1988–2026 holiday masonry grid with no line; the scroll reveal is nearly invisible.
- Guestbook: a forest band plus a column of identical white shadow cards, a comment feed rather than a book; the tribute differs only by a larger name.
- Across the page: tracked caps labels everywhere, one identical fade-and-settle in every section, about eight masses of the same weight, no peak.
- The clip poster is a social-media repost frame (burned-in caption bar, a thumbs-up "22").

What is strong and should survive: usability (large type, plain bilingual errors, 56 px buttons, visible focus, a working phone layout), carefully sourced content, strong archive photos, the Việt Nam / Đức two-sides idea of his life.

Owner's feedback: the hero, timeline UI and guestbook are boring; poor animation and transformation effects; no eye-catching aesthetic; Cormorant does not suit Vietnamese.
